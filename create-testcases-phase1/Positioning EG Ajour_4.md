### Problem
 
Construction sites are dynamic environments, constantly impacted by
changing client demands, material delivery delays, and unclear guidance
on the best solutions to use. Site managers play a central role in
coordinating all activities, ensuring that both in-house teams and
subcontractors have the information they need to complete their tasks.
They are also responsible for communicating about defects or unfinished
work with both internal teams and subcontractors, and they must follow
up to ensure issues are resolved. Additionally, site managers need to
ensure that all work is properly documented to meet client requirements
and the contractor's own standards.
 
Without a structured system to support these tasks, site managers are
often forced to work in an ad hoc, unstructured way, leading to
confusion about what has been completed and who is aware of critical
information. This results in significant stress and information overload
for site managers, making it challenging to keep projects on track.
 
### Value
 
EG Ajour enables site managers to easily capture and store final results
in a structured manner, ensuring that nothing is missed in the
documentation process. It provides a clear, straightforward overview for
creating and reporting documentation. If any questions arise later, site
managers can quickly access the necessary records for proof.
 
EG Ajour also brings all communication together, allowing the site
manager to keep all messages and updates with internal teams and
subcontractors in one place. With numerous people and tasks to track,
the site manager can access everything on their mobile device, and the
ability to pin tasks directly on a project drawing makes it easy to find
information when needed.
 
### Key features
 
Main features to showcase:
 
-   Create a task, add a picture and send to responsible person.
 
-   Show all tasks on a drawing, with colors indicating status.
 
-   List view of tasks - sort and filter to easily find the task(s) you
    are looking for.
 
-   Create a report - easy to make a report to send to someone external
    to the project, or to the platform. A couple of clicks and you have
    a pdf to send.
 
Other features highlighting document management:
 
-   Flexible folder structure: use a national standard (DK) or create
    your own.
 
#### AI
 
EG Ajour is powered by AI. AI strengthens both development and user
experience. It enables faster innovation, higher quality and software
that continues to improve as your needs evolve.
 
### Competitive landscape
 
There are a couple of competitors on the market.
 
In comparison to RIB (Byggeweb), EG Ajour has a flexible and robust
system. There are Danish standards built in, but you can also create
your own folder structure.
 
In comparison to Dalux (Dalux Field), EG Ajour is easy to set up and you
can get going within minutes.
 
EG Ajour had a stronger position a few years ago and will now improve on
a couple of aspects to regain market share.
 
There are still however many construction companies that haven\'t
digitalized, especially medium to small sized companies.
 
Main value points
 
-   **Simplicity:** Get up and running in 15 minutes.
 
-   **Time-saving:** Consolidates multiple communication and
    documentation tools, replacing WhatsApp, Excel, emails and phone
    calls.
 
-   **Reduced Overload:** Centralizes documentation, giving clear
    visibility into task statuses and responsibility assignments.
 
-   **Market Fit:** Designed for mid-to-large contractors needing a
    reliable, easy-to-implement platform to keep projects on track and
    documented.
